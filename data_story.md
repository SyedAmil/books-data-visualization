# Data Story: What do 1,000 books tell us?

**Overview.** The dataset has 1000 books priced between £10.00
and £59.99, with an average of £35.07.

**Insight 1: Ratings.** The most common rating is 1 stars, and 38% of books
are rated 4 stars or higher (see chart_ratings.png).

**Insight 2: Prices.** Most books fall in the £20-30 price band
(see chart_prices.png and chart_heatmap.png).

**Insight 3: Price vs rating.** Average price ranges only from £34.56
to £36.09 across ratings. The correlation is 0.03, so a higher
price does not mean a better rating (see chart_price_by_rating.png).

**Decision support.** Buyers should not use price as a sign of quality, and sellers
cannot expect to charge more just because a book is rated higher.
